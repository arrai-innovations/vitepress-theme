container colours in light and dark mode.

## GitHub-flavoured alerts

VitePress renders these alerts as containers. `NOTE`, `IMPORTANT`, and `CAUTION` have no `:::` equivalent.

> [!NOTE]
>
> A note alert with `inline code`.

> [!TIP]
>
> A tip alert with `inline code`.

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> An important alert with `inline code`.

> [!WARNING]
>
> A warning alert with `inline code`.

> [!CAUTION]
>
> A caution alert with `inline code`.
